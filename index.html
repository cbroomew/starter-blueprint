1 OF 3  |  EVERY MORNING 💧

CREATINE + HYDRATION

WHAT IT IS
5 g creatine + 300 mg sodium + 250 mg potassium. One stick, one drink. Creatine works through consistency. Electrolytes replace what you sweat.

WHY I TAKE IT
I live in Florida. Golf days and noon walks, I sweat buckets. The sodium keeps me hydrated so the creatine stays in the muscle. Rest days too.

2 OF 3  |  BETWEEN MEALS 🥚

WHOLE FOOD PROTEIN

WHAT IT IS
Lactose-free. No whey. 20 g from five real sources: egg, chicken, beef collagen, pea, brown rice. Built to feel like food, not a heavy shake.

WHY I TAKE IT
Whey wrecked my stomach for years. This is the first shake with zero tummy ache. Closes my protein gap on two-meal days. Supplements a meal, never replaces one.
